What it costs

$2,495 per pilgrim

It covers

  • Eight nights of lodging across the four properties
  • The shuttle from Manitowoc to Champion, and the van and support for all five walking days
  • Dave walking alongside you and leading the Twelve Steps work every day
  • Story Day and Processing Day in full
  • Most meals, your pilgrim materials, and the completion coin at Saturday's brunch

Not included: getting yourself to Manitowoc, travel insurance, meals after the farewell brunch, and anything you buy for yourself along the way.

How it's paid

  • Pay in full today, if you can. One payment, nothing to carry between now and April.
  • Or a $500 deposit today holds your place, with the balance due within 30 days.
  • Everything is paid by Thursday, January 28, 2027. No installments.
  • Cancel by email by Thursday, January 28, 2027 for a refund. If you’ve paid only the $500 deposit, it comes back in full. If you’ve paid more, everything you’ve paid comes back minus payment processing fees.
  • After January 28, 2027, your place is transferable, not refundable.
